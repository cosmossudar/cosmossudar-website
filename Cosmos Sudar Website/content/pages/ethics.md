title: Ethics & Corrections
summary: The rules this newsroom holds itself to, and how to hold it to them.
---

A one-person newsroom has no editor looking over its shoulder. That makes a written standard more important, not less. This page is that standard. It is public so that it can be used against me.

## Sources

- **Anyone can speak anonymously** if being named would put them at risk of losing a job, a benefit, a tenancy, or their safety. Where a source is anonymous, the story says why.
- **Nobody is paid** for information, ever.
- **I do not show stories to sources before publication.** I will read back direct quotations to confirm accuracy.
- **Nobody's identity is shared** with an official, a politician, or anyone else — including under pressure.

## Verification

- A claim about a place is not published unless I have been to the place.
- A number is not published unless it comes from a document, an official statement, or my own count. Where I counted it myself, the story says so, and says how.
- Where a document contradicts what an official says, both are published.
- Photographs are not staged, and are not altered beyond cropping and normal exposure correction. Any illustration or file image is labelled as such.

## The right of reply

Any individual, office or company named critically in a story is contacted for a response before publication, with a reasonable deadline. If they do not respond, the story says that they were asked and did not respond. If they respond after publication, the response is added to the story.

## Independence

- No money is accepted from any political party, candidate, or person holding public office.
- No advertising is carried on this website.
- No gift, hospitality or travel is accepted from any person or organisation that is the subject of reporting.
- If a story concerns anyone connected to me personally, that connection is disclosed inside the story.

## Corrections

Errors will happen. What matters is what happens next.

- Write to **news@cosmossudar.com** with the word **CORRECTION** in the subject line, or send it on WhatsApp.
- Tell me which sentence is wrong and, if you can, what the correct position is.
- Verified factual errors are fixed **within 24 hours** of being confirmed.
- The correction is recorded at the bottom of the story with the date, so the record is honest about having been wrong. Corrected stories are never quietly deleted.

## Takedowns

Stories are not removed because someone in authority is unhappy with them. A story will be removed or altered only where it is factually wrong and cannot be corrected, or where publication has created a genuine and demonstrable risk to a named individual's safety.

## Artificial intelligence

Software is used for transcription, translation drafts, layout and this website's code. Reporting, verification and judgement are mine. No story on this site is generated by a machine, and no image on this site is generated by a machine unless it is explicitly labelled as an illustration.
