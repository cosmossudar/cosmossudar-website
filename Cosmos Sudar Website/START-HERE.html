Step 1 Change two things first

Before anything goes online, two details in the folder are placeholders and need your real information.

⚠ Your WhatsApp number is fake right now

Open the file site.json in this folder (double-click it; if it asks which app, choose TextEdit or Notepad). Find this line:

"whatsapp_number": "919999999999",

Replace it with your real number — country code first, digits only, no plus sign, no spaces. For an Indian number 98765 43210, you would write "whatsapp_number": "919876543210",

Do the same for "instagram", "youtube" and "twitter" — put your real profile links there, or leave the quotes empty like "" if you do not have one. Those buttons then simply do not appear.

Careful: keep every quotation mark and comma exactly where it is. Those are the only rule in that file.

Delete the sample stories when you are ready

Inside content/news/ there are five stories with sample in the file name. They exist so the website does not look empty while you set it up. Delete them once you have written two or three of your own. Leave _TEMPLATE.md alone — it is your blank form, and it never appears on the website.

Step 2 Make a free GitHub account

GitHub is where your website will live. Think of it as a free filing cabinet that also happens to publish to the internet.

  1. Go to github.com/signup
  2. Use news@cosmossudar.com as the email, so everything sits under your own domain.
  3. Pick a username. Something like cosmossudar. It will be visible, so keep it clean.
  4. Choose the Free plan when it asks. Never choose anything else. If a page ever asks for a card, you have taken a wrong turn — go back.
  5. Confirm the email they send you.

about 5 minutes

Step 3 Upload your website

  1. Signed in to GitHub, click the + at the top right → New repository.
  2. Repository name: cosmossudar-website
  3. Set it to Public. This matters — free hosting and free rebuilds only apply to public projects. Public means people can read the code, not that they can change it. Only you can change it.
  4. Do not tick "Add a README file". Leave all the tick-boxes empty.
  5. Click Create repository.
  6. On the next screen click the link uploading an existing file.
  7. Open this folder on your computer. Select everything inside it — the assets, content and .github folders, plus build.py, site.json, .pages.yml, CNAME and .gitignore. Drag them all onto the GitHub page.
  8. Wait for the upload bars to finish, then click Commit changes at the bottom.

If the .github folder refuses to upload

Folders starting with a dot are hidden on Mac and Windows, and browsers sometimes skip them. If .github did not make it, create it by hand: click Add file → Create new file, and in the name box type exactly .github/workflows/publish.yml — typing the slashes creates the folders for you. Then open .github/workflows/publish.yml from your folder in TextEdit or Notepad, copy everything, paste it in, and commit.

On a Mac you can reveal hidden files in Finder by pressing Command + Shift + Full stop.

Step 4 Switch the website on

  1. In your repository, click Settings (top row of tabs).
  2. In the left sidebar, click Pages.
  3. Under Source, open the dropdown and choose GitHub Actions. Not "Deploy from a branch" — GitHub Actions.
  4. Click the Actions tab at the top. You will see a job called "Publish website" running. Give it about a minute to turn into a green tick.
  5. Go back to Settings → Pages. Your website address is now shown at the top. Click it. Your site is live.

At this point the site works at an address like cosmossudar.github.io/cosmossudar-website. The next step gives it your real name.

Step 5 Point cosmossudar.com at it

Two halves: tell GitHub your domain, then tell your domain about GitHub.

Half one — on GitHub

  1. Settings → Pages again.
  2. Under Custom domain, type cosmossudar.com and click Save.

Half two — where your domain is registered

Sign in wherever you bought or manage cosmossudar.com. If it came bundled with your Google Workspace subscription, that will usually be Squarespace Domains (which took over Google Domains) — check your original purchase email. Find the section called DNS, DNS records or Manage custom records, and add these eight records.

TypeName / HostValue / Points to
A@185.199.108.153
A@185.199.109.153
A@185.199.110.153
A@185.199.111.153
AAAA@2606:50c0:8000::153
AAAA@2606:50c0:8001::153
AAAA@2606:50c0:8002::153
AAAA@2606:50c0:8003::153

Then add one more so that www.cosmossudar.com also works:

TypeName / HostValue / Points to
CNAMEwwwYOUR-USERNAME.github.io

Replace YOUR-USERNAME with the GitHub username you chose. Note there is no repository name in that value — just the username, then .github.io.

⚠ Do not touch your MX records

Your MX records are what deliver mail to news@cosmossudar.com. The records above are A, AAAA and CNAME records — completely separate. Add the new ones, change nothing else, and your Google email keeps working exactly as it does today.

If your registrar has already put a default A record or a "parking" / "forwarding" record on @, delete that one. It will fight with these.

  1. Save the records and wait. Usually 15 minutes to an hour; the outside limit is 24 hours.
  2. Come back to Settings → Pages. Once GitHub shows a green tick beside your domain, tick Enforce HTTPS. That gives you the padlock, free. If the tick-box is greyed out, it is not ready yet — check again in a few hours.

15 minutes of work, then waiting

Step 6 Set up your writing panel

This is what replaces Wix's editor. A proper form with a photo upload button, free, that works on your phone as well as your laptop.

  1. Go to pagescms.org and click Sign in with GitHub.
  2. Allow it to see your repositories. When it asks which, choose only cosmossudar-website — not all of them.
  3. Open that project. It reads the .pages.yml file already in your folder, so your form appears ready-made with the right boxes: Headline, Date, Section, Place, Summary, Main photo, and the story itself.
  4. Bookmark that page on your phone's home screen. That is your newsroom now.

You are never locked in

Pages CMS is one convenient door, not the building. Your stories are ordinary text files sitting in your own GitHub account. If Pages CMS ever shuts down or starts charging, you lose nothing — you edit the same files directly on GitHub's own website, or switch to any other tool. That is the difference between this and Wix.

Step 7 Publish a story

Your daily routine, start to finish:

  1. Open your Pages CMS bookmark.
  2. Click News Stories → Add new.
  3. Fill in the headline, pick the date and section, and write a two-sentence summary. The summary is what people see in the WhatsApp preview when your story is shared — spend a moment on it.
  4. Upload your main photo. Landscape photos look best. Straight from the phone is fine.
  5. Write the story in the big box at the bottom.
  6. Click Save.
  7. Wait about a minute. The story is live on cosmossudar.com. Nothing else to press.

Writing it directly on GitHub instead

If you would rather skip the panel: go to your repository, open content/news/, click Add file → Create new file, and name it in this pattern — 2026-08-12-water-shortage-poonamallee.md. Copy the contents of _TEMPLATE.md as your starting point. Both routes produce exactly the same result.

Keeping something as a draft

Turn on Keep as draft in the panel (or write draft: true at the top of the file). The story stays invisible to the public until you turn it off. Useful for writing over several days.

Making a correction

Open the story, fix the sentence, save. It updates within a minute. Your published ethics page promises corrections are noted at the bottom of the story with a date — add a line like **Correction, 14 August 2026:** an earlier version said 43 acres. The figure is 34 acres. at the end.

Step 8 When something goes wrong

I published, but nothing changed on the website

Go to your repository → Actions tab. Look at the most recent run. A green tick means it published — try refreshing the page with Ctrl+Shift+R (or Cmd+Shift+R on Mac) to clear the old copy from your browser. A red cross means the build failed; click it to see which story it stopped on. It is almost always a missing --- line, or a date written in the wrong shape. Fix that story and it publishes itself.

The site is up but the design is missing

Usually the assets folder did not upload fully. Check that assets/css/style.css exists in your repository.

cosmossudar.com shows an error or someone else's page

DNS has not finished spreading, or an old parking record is still in place. Wait a few hours, then re-check that no leftover A record points somewhere else.

My email stopped working

Then an MX record was changed or deleted. Go back to your DNS page and restore Google's MX records — Google Workspace admin help has the exact list. The records in this guide never touch mail.

Seeing the site before it goes online

Open the _site folder inside this folder and double-click index.html. The whole website opens in your browser — every page, every link, working, with nothing published and no internet needed. Do this now, before you start Step 2.

The only part that will not work offline is the search box on the All Stories page, which needs a real web address. Everything else is exactly what visitors will see.
